Our story began long before the name existed

Founder Haley Finlay grew up in her family’s CPA firm, where she started working at just 10 years old. What began as helping around the office grew into a career, and years later, she took over the family business.

That experience gave her an appreciation for the relationships, trust, and personal connection that make a small accounting firm so much more than a place to prepare taxes. It shaped her vision for Ledgerloom: a firm where clients feel known, questions are welcome, and good service means more than meeting deadlines.

Founded in Birmingham, Alabama, Ledgerloom is both a continuation of that family legacy and the beginning of something new.
